// 2015 Q3 \\

Deep in the heart of Ravenwood Manor, a haunted gallery comes alive every Halloween night.
Each year, the Curator of Shadows prepares an eerie exhibition of cursed artifacts created by the realm’s most infamous dark artists:

A — Armand the Alchemist, master of potions and metallic sculptures.

B — Beatrix the Bonecarver, whose skeletal creations rattle softly in the night.

C — Corvus the Crowmage, painter of black-feathered nightmares.

D — Delilah the Dollmaker, known for her unsettling porcelain works that sometimes… move.

The curator must arrange these cursed artifacts in a row inside the Haunted Gallery.
However, because each artist’s works are infused with identical dark magic, all pieces by the same artist are indistinguishable.

For instance, suppose the exhibition includes:

1 work by Armand (A)

2 by Beatrix (B)

1 by Corvus (C)

Then the possible arrangements of the artifacts (each represented by their artist’s initial) are:

ABBC
ABCB
ACBB
BABC
BACB
BBAC
BBCA
BCAB
BCBA
CABB
CBAB
CBBA


These are listed in alphabetical order, as demanded by the curator’s ancient spellbook of order.

💀 Your Task

Write a program to determine the n-th possible arrangement of the Haunted Gallery.

Your program should input five integers:
a, b, c, d, n

where:

a, b, c, and d are the number of works by Armand, Beatrix, Corvus, and Delilah respectively (each between 0 and 5, inclusive).

n is the number (1 ≤ n ≤ 234) representing which arrangement the Curator wishes to summon.

At least one artist will have contributed at least one piece, and n will never exceed the total number of possible arrangements.

🎃 Output

Your program should output a single string — the n-th arrangement of cursed artworks in lexicographical order (A < B < C < D).
