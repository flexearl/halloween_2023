🕯️ Story

It’s Halloween night in the village of Hallowdale, and you’ve been tasked with counting how many pumpkins are glowing in the streets.
Each house on the street puts out one decoration — it could be a pumpkin (🎃), a ghost (👻), or a bat (🦇).

The mayor needs to know how many pumpkins are on display tonight so she can judge the Best Decorated Street competition.

💀 Your Task

Write a program that counts how many pumpkins appear in a string of decorations.

🎃 Input

A single line containing a string of emojis or letters representing decorations.

Each decoration is represented by one character:

P for Pumpkin

G for Ghost

B for Bat

The string length is between 1 and 50.

🦇 Output

A single integer — the number of pumpkins (P) in the input.
